## Chapter 8: Full Exam Simulation and Test-Taking Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | NCA-AIIO exam format: number of questions, time limit, delivery method? | 50 questions, 90 minutes, online remote-proctored. Equals 1.8 minutes per question average. Exam tip: You can flag and review questions, so skip hard ones initially and return with remaining time. |
| 2 | NCA-AIIO passing score and what does it mean in absolute terms? | Approximately 70% = 35 out of 50 questions correct. This means you can miss 15 questions and still pass. Exam tip: Don't panic over hard questions - you have a 15-question buffer. |
| 3 | NCA-AIIO domain weights: which two domains are highest weighted and what are their percentages? | Infrastructure: 40% (~20 questions), Essential AI: 38% (~19 questions). Together = 78% of exam. Exam tip: Prioritize studying these domains, but don't skip Platform (22%) - it's still ~11 questions. |
| 4 | Time management strategy: you have 15 questions left with 20 minutes remaining. Are you on pace? | No, you're behind pace. 15 questions × 1.8 min = 27 minutes needed, but you have 20 minutes. You're 7 minutes behind. Strategy: Flag hard questions, answer easy ones quickly, use elimination on flagged questions. |
| 5 | What are the three most common wrong answer patterns in NCA-AIIO scenario questions? | 1) Over-engineering - adding unnecessary complexity/features, 2) Cost-insensitive - choosing most expensive without justification, 3) Outdated tech - using deprecated products when modern alternatives exist. Exam tip: When in doubt, choose simpler, cost-appropriate, current solutions. |
| 6 | Elimination technique for scenario questions: what's the correct order of steps? | 1) List ALL hard requirements from question, 2) Eliminate answers missing ANY requirement, 3) Apply soft preferences (cost, complexity, performance) to remaining options. Never eliminate based on preferences before validating all requirements met. |
| 7 | In nvidia-smi troubleshooting questions, what does 'Memory 95%, GPU 15%' indicate? | Memory bottleneck - GPU is starved waiting for data. Common causes: batch size too large, inefficient data loading, insufficient CPU preprocessing. Exam tip: Look for MISMATCHED utilization percentages - high memory + low GPU = memory issue, high GPU + low memory = compute bound. |
| 8 | When should you change a flagged answer during review vs. leaving your first choice? | Change if you can apply elimination technique or recall studied knowledge that wasn't accessible during first attempt. DON'T change on gut feeling alone. Exam tip: 'First instinct' myth doesn't apply to technical exams - applying test-taking strategy during review improves scores. |
| 9 | Service selection question: when comparing NVIDIA GPUs for cost-sensitive inference workloads, which GPU line is typically the wrong answer? | H100 - it's optimized for training and high-performance computing, not cost-efficient inference. For inference cost optimization, look to L40S or A10. Exam tip: H100 appearing in 'performance-per-dollar' or 'cost optimization' questions is usually a distractor. |
| 10 | Capacity planning with overhead: calculate GPUs needed for 60,000 req/hr with 25% overhead if each GPU handles 3,000 req/hr? | Total capacity needed: 60,000 × 1.25 = 75,000 req/hr. GPUs: 75,000 / 3,000 = 25 GPUs exactly. Formula: (Required throughput × Overhead multiplier) / Per-unit capacity. Exam tip: Calculate total needed capacity INCLUDING overhead FIRST, then divide. |
| 11 | What does 'Budget is not a primary concern' mean in scenario questions - and what does it NOT mean? | MEANS: Don't eliminate options based on cost, focus on meeting requirements. DOESN'T MEAN: Pick the most expensive option. Exam tests appropriate solutions, not maximum spending. Cost-insensitive ≠ cost-wasteful. |
| 12 | Review strategy with 10 minutes left and 6 flagged questions: prioritize by domain weight or by question type? | By question type - focus on heavily tested types (scenario-based, service selection) where you can apply elimination technique. Domain weight affects study priority, not review strategy. A correct Platform answer = same points as Infrastructure answer. |
